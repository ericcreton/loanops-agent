RIVERSIDE PLAZA
COMMERCIAL MORTGAGE LOAN AGREEMENT

Loan ID: 1001
Borrower: Riverside Holdings LLC
Property: Riverside Plaza

SECTION 4 — MATURITY

The outstanding principal balance and all accrued amounts shall
become due and payable on June 1, 2031.

SECTION 8 — FINANCIAL COVENANTS

The Borrower shall maintain a Debt Service Coverage Ratio
of no less than 1.20.

Debt Service Coverage Ratio shall be determined using annual
Net Operating Income divided by annual debt service.

SECTION 11 — ADDITIONAL INDEBTEDNESS

The Borrower may not incur additional secured indebtedness
against Riverside Plaza without the prior written consent
of the lender.

SECTION 15 — INSURANCE

The Borrower must maintain property, casualty, and liability
insurance for the mortgaged property.
